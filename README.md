# Pom's Big Bloom

Survive 12 waves, grow huge, beat King Grump. A cute voxel wave-survival game made for Hack Club Haven.

**Play in your browser:** https://itzpranav101.github.io/pom-big-bloom/

- WASD to move. Your weapons fire by themselves.
- Grab gold seeds to level up, then pick one of three upgrades.
- Spend seeds in the shop between waves. Every 4th wave is a boss.
- Dying earns Sun Petals for permanent upgrades and new characters.

Mouse look, scroll to zoom, Shift to roll, Space to jump, Esc to pause. Best with a mouse and keyboard and headphones.
