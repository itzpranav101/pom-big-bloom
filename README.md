# Pom's Big Bloom

Loop the Grumps, bloom the meadow, beat King Grump. A cute voxel arena game made for Hack Club Haven.

**Play in your browser:** https://itzpranav101.github.io/pom-big-bloom/

- The Grumps have wilted the meadow. Walk off your flowers and Pom trails a vine.
- Get back onto your flowers, or cross your own vine, to close the loop. Everything inside blooms into real 3D flowers, and every Grump caught in it pops.
- Bloom enough of the meadow to clear each wave. The bar over Pom is how much vine you have left.
- 16 kinds of Grump: some eat your flowers, dash at your vine, tunnel underground, steal seeds or hatch from nests. Three bosses each fight differently.
- 20 heroes, each with a power that changes how loops play, unlocked through 39 challenges that also unlock 20 hats.
- Click to fire a heavy shot, hold for the machine gun.
- **Finger gun:** press H, allow the camera, and aim with your hand. Drop your thumb to fire, hold it for the machine gun. Hand tracking runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.
- The music follows the action: it builds while you draw a vine and lands every loop on the beat.
- After your first win, Thorn levels 1 to 8 make runs harder for more Sun Petals.

WASD to move, mouse to look, scroll to zoom, Shift to roll, Space to jump, Esc to pause. On a tablet or phone, drag on the left to move and touch the right to fire. Best with headphones.
