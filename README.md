# Pom's Big Bloom

Loop the Grumps, bloom the meadow, beat King Grump. A cute voxel arena game made for Hack Club Haven.

**Play in your browser:** https://itzpranav101.github.io/pom-big-bloom/

- The Grumps have wilted the meadow. Walk off your flowers and Pom trails a vine.
- Get back onto your flowers, or cross your own vine, to close the loop. Everything inside blooms, and every Grump caught in it pops.
- Bloom enough of the meadow to clear each wave. The bar over Pom is how much vine you have left.
- Spiky and big Grumps snap your vine; small ones get pricked. Your flowers heal you.
- Weapons fire by themselves. Gold seeds level you up; pick one of three upgrades. Every 4th wave is a boss: loop it to wrap it.
- Dying earns Sun Petals for permanent upgrades and new characters.

WASD to move, mouse to look, scroll to zoom, Shift to roll, Space to jump, Esc to pause. Best with a mouse and keyboard and headphones.
